# Case Study — Distressed Property Detection

## Problem
In the hyper-competitive real estate investment market, the ability to act first is the difference between a multi-million dollar acquisition and a missed opportunity. The client’s team was manually reviewing hundreds of listings daily, a process that was slow, inconsistent, and often resulted in identifying deals long after they had been claimed by competitors.

## Solution
We engineered an AI-driven detection engine that acts as a 24/7 "digital scout." By combining Vision AI for physical assessment and NLP for linguistic analysis, the system identifies distressed properties with 90%+ accuracy. The integration of public records added a layer of financial verification that was previously handled manually by specialized analysts.

## Impact
- **Speed to Market:** Alert latency was reduced from 24–48 hours to under 5 minutes.
- **Efficiency:** The acquisition team now only reviews high-probability "distressed" deals, effectively tripling their throughput.
- **Competitive Advantage:** Speed became the primary competitive edge, allowing the client to secure offers before competitors even identified the listing.

## Engineering Approach
- **Multi-Modal Signal Fusion:** Designing a scoring system that balances disparate data types (images vs. text vs. financial records).
- **Asynchronous Architecture:** Building for speed by processing vision and text components in parallel.
- **Robust Error Handling:** Ensuring the pipeline remains operational during API outages with sophisticated retry logic.
